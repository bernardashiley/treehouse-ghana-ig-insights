\section*{Executive Summary}
The public profile dataset shows 27,386 followers at collection time and includes 135 owned feed posts, 84 reels, 21 third-party mentions, and 106 comments from selected top posts.
The analysis covers 135 posts, 84 reels, 21 mentions, and 106 comments.

\section*{Recommendations}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Post more of the strongest occasion-led pillars.
\item Use reels for discovery and add direct booking prompts.
\item Turn repeated comments into caption FAQs and highlights.
\item Track public engagement score by pillar monthly alongside native analytics.
\end{enumerate}
\section*{Limitations and Ethics}
This report is based on public Instagram data analysis. It excludes private Instagram analytics such as impressions, reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, and DMs. Conclusions are directional, not absolute.
